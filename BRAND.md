## 1. Executive Summary

Fullyo.io is Sebastien L'Heureux's short-term rental and hospitality technology company. The business helps hosts, property managers, boutique hotels, and hospitality operators move beyond simple listings by building the infrastructure needed for stronger OTA visibility, direct bookings, brand control, Guesty/PMS operations, automation, and long-term growth.

Fullyo.io builds hospitality brands and booking systems — not just attractive websites. The goal is to help clients move closer to a balanced 50/50 revenue mix between direct bookings and OTAs, while still using OTAs strategically as acquisition channels.

- **Tagline:** The Property Growth Agency
- **Core promise themes:** fully equipped, fully supported, fully in control
- **Core platform partner:** Guesty (especially Guesty Pro)
- **Primary market:** short-term rental hosts, boutique hospitality operators, property managers, direct-booking operators
- **Primary outcome:** build brands, increase direct bookings, improve OTA visibility, centralize operations, automate guest flows, reduce platform dependency

## 5. The Five Pillars — Core Framework

Fullyo's strategic framework for maximizing short-term rental performance through data, technology, and marketing. Each pillar shows how Fullyo is different from generic tools, regular agencies, or standard PMS onboarding.

### Pillar 1 — Strategic Analysis & Blueprint
**Result:** 28.5% average performance increase.

We conduct a deep dive into the business — identifying what works and what doesn't, defining the ideal guest avatar, and uncovering key improvement areas. Most hosts only use basic AI tools or surface-level listing checks. Fullyo brings deeper industry knowledge, STR-specific experience, and better tools.

**What's included:**
- Identify revenue leaks in your current listing
- Competitor price benchmarking analysis
- Ideal Guest Avatar definition
- Custom Performance Blueprint creation
- Analysis of positioning, OTA visibility, booking flow, brand strength, guest journey, pricing logic, property setup, direct-booking potential

**Problem → Solution:**
| Problem | Fullyo Solution |
|---|---|
| Strategic decisions made without reliable market data | Professional-grade data and market intelligence |
| Optimization relies on trial and error | AI leveraged to surface patterns and opportunities |
| No external validation of pricing/positioning | Industry experts apply proven strategies |

---

### Pillar 2 — Direct Booking Website & Brand System
**Result:** 15–35% more reservations · Up to 18% margin lift.

Regular web agencies don't understand STR deeply enough. Fullyo specializes only in short-term rental and hospitality. We build brands, not listings. The website is part of a complete booking system — not just a pretty design. Fullyo handles API integration and booking-engine connection; **Guesty remains the operational source of truth** for rates, availability, reservations, and booking logic.

**What's included:**
- Seamless PMS integration
- Automated upsells & add-ons
- Mobile-first & speed optimized
- Full guest data capture (email/phone)
- Designed around conversion, trust, brand positioning, guest education, professional direct-booking flow

**Problem → Solution:**
| Problem | Fullyo Solution |
|---|---|
| 15–25% commission leakage per booking | Commission-free direct bookings that protect margins |
| Guest data controlled by platforms | Full ownership of guest data for future remarketing |
| Visibility depends on platform algorithms | Independent channel, unaffected by algorithm changes |
| No brand presence outside marketplaces | Standalone branded site for direct conversion |

---

### Pillar 3 — Multi-Channel Strategy & OTA Distribution
**Result:** 5 channels minimum, broader guest reach.

The Airbnb-only era is over for serious operators. We ensure visibility on at least five key channels where ideal guests search. Multi-channel distribution differentiates from standard PMS onboarding — PMS onboarding shows the software; it doesn't build the distribution strategy or complete OTA setup.

**What's included:**
- Sync across Airbnb, VRBO, Booking.com, Google Vacation Rentals, Expedia
- Niche platform access (Glamping Hub, pet-friendly, etc.)
- Zero double-bookings guarantee
- Centralized calendar management
- Reach guests who never search Airbnb

**Full platform reach:** Agoda · VRBO · Homes & Villas by Marriott Bonvoy · GlampingHub · Expedia · Booking.com · Airbnb · Google Vacation Rentals · Hopper Homes · Trip.com · bnbfinder · Plum Guide

**Problem → Solution:**
| Problem | Fullyo Solution |
|---|---|
| Distribution limited to one or two platforms | Distributed across 5+ major booking channels |
| Channel choice reflects operator preference | Strategy aligns with actual guest booking behavior |
| Single-platform dependence = vulnerability | Diversified exposure reduces volatility and risk |
| Demand missed from other platforms | Broader reach captures additional bookings |

### Pillar 4 — Guesty Command Center, Onboarding & Automation
**Result:** Save 4–12+ hours per week.

We centralize the entire operation into one powerful dashboard. Fullyo goes deeper than Guesty standard onboarding. Guesty provides 5 sessions of ~50 minutes. **Fullyo provides 5 sessions of 1.5 hours for hosts and 6 sessions of 1.5 hours for property managers.** Extra sessions available when needed.

Fullyo doesn't just show the software. We help **set up the business**: properties, calendars, channels, booking flows, automations, messaging, guest forms, agreements, deposits, owner workflows, operational structure.

**What's included:**
- All-in-one dashboard
- Unified inbox across all channels
- Automated guest messaging
- Cleaner & maintenance scheduling
- Dynamic pricing tool integration
- Zero double-bookings across all platforms

**Problem → Solution:**
| Problem | Fullyo Solution |
|---|---|
| Multiple platforms require separate management | Single command center connecting all channels |
| Manual updates increase errors/inefficiency | Automated sync across calendars and messaging |
| Disconnected systems = operational stress | Streamlined workflows designed to scale |
| Higher risk of double bookings | Real-time updates across all listings |

### Pillar 5 — Direct Booking Growth Blueprint
**Result:** 10–30% increase in direct bookings.

Fullyo helps hosts generate direct bookings — something PMS companies don't meaningfully do. The last onboarding session is dedicated to direct-booking generation and post-launch system usage. The larger goal: move clients closer to a **50/50 balance** between direct bookings and OTA bookings.

**What's included:**
- Email marketing campaigns to past guests
- Social media retargeting ads
- Shoulder season calendar filling
- Repeat guest loyalty programs
- Organic social strategy, referral tactics, Google visibility, paid ads

**Problem → Solution:**
| Problem | Fullyo Solution |
|---|---|
| No structured marketing foundation | Direct booking growth strategy blueprint |
| Marketing efforts minimal or ad hoc | Clear short-, mid-, long-term growth strategies |
| Past guests not leveraged | Systematic use of owned guest data |
| Demand relies on third-party platforms | Direct demand generation outside marketplaces |

- Fullyo differentiates from standard Guesty onboarding by spending time **setting up the business**, not just demoing the software.

## 9. Sales, Onboarding & Client Flow

1. Sale completed and payment confirmed before onboarding is scheduled (where possible).
2. Client receives 5 or 6 scheduled onboarding sessions based on host vs. property manager.
3. **Hosts:** 5 sessions × 1.5 hours.
4. **Property managers:** 6 sessions × 1.5 hours.
5. Extra sessions available if needed.
6. Guesty/PMS setup, channel strategy, automation, booking flow, and operational structure completed during onboarding.
7. **Website delivery happens at the end of onboarding,** once core booking infrastructure is ready.
8. **Last session is dedicated to direct-booking growth** — how to generate bookings and use the system after launch.
9. Post-onboarding: Fullyo continues supporting clients as they grow.

### 4-Step Client Journey (Public-Facing)
1. **Strategy Call** — No sales pressure. Just strategy.
2. **Custom Plan** — A performance blueprint for the property.
3. **System Build** — Implementation of all relevant pillars.
4. **Growth** — Ongoing optimization and marketing execution.

### Onboarding Speed
- Same week: strategy call scheduled.
- As little as 2 weeks: full system live.
