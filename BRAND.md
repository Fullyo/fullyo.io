## 1. Executive Summary

Fullyo.io is Sebastien L'Heureux's short-term rental and hospitality technology company. The business helps hosts, property managers, boutique hotels, and hospitality operators move beyond simple listings by building the infrastructure needed for stronger OTA visibility, direct bookings, brand control, Guesty/PMS operations, automation, and long-term growth.

**Fullyo.io is not positioned as a regular web agency.** The company builds hospitality brands and booking systems, not just attractive websites. The goal is to help clients move closer to a balanced 50/50 revenue mix between direct bookings and OTAs, while still using OTAs strategically as acquisition channels.

- **Core promise themes:** fully equipped, fully supported, fully in control
- **Core platform partner:** Guesty (especially Guesty Pro)
- **Primary market:** short-term rental hosts, boutique hospitality operators, property managers, direct-booking operators
- **Primary outcome:** build brands, increase direct bookings, improve OTA visibility, centralize operations, automate guest flows, reduce platform dependency

## 5. The Five Pillars — Core Framework

Fullyo uses five pillars to explain the complete system. Each pillar shows how Fullyo is different from generic tools, regular agencies, or standard PMS onboarding.

### Pillar 1 — Strategic Analysis & Blueprint
- Most hosts only use basic AI tools or surface-level listing checks. Fullyo brings deeper industry knowledge, STR-specific experience, and better tools to identify what is really holding performance back.
- The analysis looks at positioning, OTA visibility, booking flow, brand strength, guest journey, pricing logic, property setup, and direct-booking potential.
- The goal is a practical blueprint for building a stronger booking ecosystem — not generic recommendations.

### Pillar 2 — Direct Booking Website & Brand System
- Regular web agencies don't understand STR deeply enough. Fullyo specializes only in short-term rental and hospitality businesses.
- **Fullyo builds brands, not listings.** The website is part of a complete booking system, not just a pretty design.
- Fullyo handles API integration and booking-engine connection where needed; **Guesty remains the operational source of truth** for rates, availability, reservations, and booking logic.
- The website is designed around conversion, trust, brand positioning, guest education, and a professional direct-booking flow.

### Pillar 3 — Multi-Channel Strategy & OTA Distribution
- Fullyo helps clients get listed and positioned across OTAs: Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo, Google Vacation Rentals, and other relevant channels.
- This is a major differentiator from standard PMS onboarding — PMS onboarding shows the software; it doesn't fully build the distribution strategy or complete OTA setup.
- The goal: use OTAs as visibility engines while building a stronger direct-booking brand behind them.

### Pillar 4 — Guesty Command Center, Onboarding & Automation
- Fullyo goes deeper than Guesty standard onboarding. Guesty provides 5 sessions of ~50 minutes; **Fullyo provides 5 sessions of 1.5 hours for hosts and 6 sessions of 1.5 hours for property managers.**
- Extra sessions are available when needed.
- Fullyo doesn't only show the software. Fullyo helps **set up the business**: properties, calendars, channels, booking flows, automations, messaging, guest forms, agreements, deposits, owner workflows, operational structure.
- After onboarding, Fullyo keeps supporting clients to grow, improve, and automate.

### Pillar 5 — Direct Booking Growth Blueprint
- Fullyo helps hosts generate direct bookings — something PMS companies do not meaningfully do.
- The last onboarding session focuses on how to generate direct bookings and how to use the system after launch.
- The blueprint can include: organic social strategy, referral tactics, email/data capture, repeat guest strategy, Google visibility, paid ads, social media execution.
- Larger goal: move clients closer to a **50/50 balance between direct bookings and OTA bookings.**

## 9. Sales, Onboarding & Client Flow

1. Sale completed and payment confirmed before onboarding is scheduled (where possible).
2. Client receives 5 or 6 scheduled onboarding sessions (depending on host vs. property manager).
3. **Hosts:** 5 sessions × 1.5 hours.
4. **Property managers:** 6 sessions × 1.5 hours.
5. Extra sessions available if needed.
6. Guesty/PMS setup, channel strategy, automation, booking flow, and operational structure are completed during onboarding.
7. **Website delivery happens at the end of onboarding,** once the core booking infrastructure is ready.
8. **Last session is dedicated to direct-booking growth** — how to generate bookings and use the system after launch.
9. Post-onboarding: Fullyo continues supporting clients as they grow, improve, and automate.

## 14. Voice & Tone Rules

- Confident, direct, operator-to-operator. Premium without being corporate.
- Empathetic to host pain points — but **never fear-based.**
- Focus on control, growth, support, opportunity.
- Lead with outcomes, not features.

### Signature Lines
- "Fully equipped. Fully supported. Fully in control."
- "We build brands, not listings."
- "We help set up the business, not just show you the software."
- "The website, PMS, automations, booking engine, guest flow, and channel strategy all work together."
- "Owners keep control of their pricing, brand, and guest relationship."

### Words to Favor
infrastructure · system · blueprint · command center · operating system · hospitality · direct booking · partner · concierge · ownership · growth

### Words / Phrases to Avoid
- "Solution," "platform" (generic SaaS framing)
- "Revolutionary," "disruptive"
- Anything that frames Fullyo as a self-serve software product
- Fear-based framings against OTAs (e.g., "the Airbnb-only era is over" violates the rule)
- "Agency" as the primary self-description
